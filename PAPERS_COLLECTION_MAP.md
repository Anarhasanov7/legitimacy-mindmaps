## 1. THE FOUNDATIONS — the definitions everyone cites
*Without these, nothing else exists. Cite them once, early.*

### Suchman 1995 — "Managing Legitimacy" (2 copies)
- **The definition:** legitimacy = "generalized perception that actions are desirable, proper, appropriate within a socially constructed system"
- **The 3 types:** pragmatic (useful to me?) · moral (right thing?) · cognitive (looks normal?)
- **For you:** THE anchor. Your backers perform all three judgments; verifiability is the pragmatic test

### Zimmerman & Zeitz 2002 — "Beyond Survival"
- **The question:** why do new ventures die ("liability of newness")?
- **The finding:** legitimacy is a *resource that unlocks other resources* — funding, customers, partners
- **For you:** Kickstarter success = legitimacy unlocking capital, literally their mechanism at scale

### Deephouse cluster (4 papers)
- Deephouse — "Organizational Legitimacy: Six Key Questions" — the field's FAQ
- Deephouse & Carter 2005 — legitimacy ≠ reputation (different constructs!)
- Deephouse & Suchman 2008/2012 — legitimacy inside institutionalism
- Deephouse 2023 Lausanne slides — the updated view
- **For you:** use Deephouse&Carter to separate legitimacy from "reputation" when reviewers push the rival

### Ruef & Scott 1998 — hospital survival
- **What it did:** measured legitimacy *dimensions* (regulative + normative) and showed they independently predict survival
- **For you:** the template for your multi-dimension coding (pragmatic/moral/cognitive/regulative)

### The primers
- Suddaby, Bitektine & Haack 2015 — "Legitimacy" — the Oxford-style handbook chapter
- legitimacy_review.pdf · legitimacy_theory_table8.pdf — synthetic tables

## 2. THE EVALUATOR SIDE — how judges actually judge ★ YOUR MECHANISM
*The micro-foundations: what happens inside a backer's head. This is YOUR lane.*

### Bitektine & Haack 2015 — "The macro and the micro"
- **The move:** legitimacy has two levels — institutional validity (what society already accepts) + individual propriety (what THIS judge thinks)
- **For you:** explains staff_pick — a validity cue that pre-loads the judgment before the backer evaluates

### Bitektine et al. 2020 — micro-level measurement
- **What:** how to actually measure individual legitimacy judgments

### Haack, Schilke & Zucker 2020 — "Legitimacy Revisited" ★
- **The three-way split:** PROPRIETY (my judgment) · VALIDITY (institutional endorsement) · CONSENSUS (everyone else's judgment)
- **For you:** your dataset has all three — verifiable claims→propriety, staff_pick→validity, sustained stranger-funding→consensus. Cancelled campaigns = validity–consensus gap collapse

### Haack & Sieweke 2020 — first-order vs second-order judgments
- **What:** judging the org (1st order) vs judging the judges (2nd order) — backers watch each other

### Haack et al. 2026 — G20 multi-level
- **What:** propriety/validity/consensus measured across contexts — newest measurement

### bitektine-song-2022 — institutional logics, pricing & CSR
- **What:** evaluators apply different logics; pricing context changes legitimacy judgments
- **For you:** category heterogeneity — gamers vs patrons judge by different logics

### Ivanova & Ruffo 2018 — SME legitimacy judgements
- **What:** how observers judge small-firm legitimacy — closest to your creator-level judgments
